Why the mix matters

Variety is the product.

A dining hall feeds you. CraveU lets you choose. We fill bays deliberately: a few national brands for the people who know what they want, and more local favorites for the people who want something they can't get anywhere else on campus. Each semester we review sales and student feedback and rotate the weakest bay.
