Our story

Built by students who were tired of the line.

CraveU started in a BTE 210 group chat with a very specific complaint: twenty minutes between classes is enough time to eat, but not enough time to wait, walk and find a table. So we designed the place we wished existed.

Why a kitchen hall, not another restaurant?

Campus dining has a variety problem and a time problem. The spots that are good are slow at noon; the spots that are fast are the same every week. Opening one more restaurant wouldn't fix either.

A takeout-only kitchen hall does. Eight small kitchens under one roof, one ordering app, one pickup counter. Restaurants get a cheap way onto campus. Students get real choice and real speed. And because every bay is built for takeout, the whole place is tuned for the way a school day actually runs.

We used AI as a co-founder on everything from the name to this website. Every decision in it, from the single pickup counter to the flat service fee, is ours and ties back to the business model.

The brand

Why the U has a bite out of it

The varsity block U in Campus Green and Crave Orange makes CraveU feel like it belongs on campus. The bite says food and cravings at a glance. Heavy slab type stays legible on a phone, a bag, and a sign across the street.
