The group-order problem, solved

One friend wants a burger. One wants tacos. One wants a bowl.

Everybody still eats together. Start a group order in the app, share the link, and each person adds their own food from any bay and pays their own way. It all lands at the same pickup counter at the same time.

  • Each person pays separately, with their own Dining Dollars or card
  • Orders from different kitchens are timed to finish together
  • One order number, one counter, one trip
