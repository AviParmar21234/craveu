What you get

Everything but the food.

01

A takeout-ready bay

Ventilated bay with hood, fire suppression, hand sink, prep surface and shared walk-in and dry storage. Bring your specialty equipment; everything else is already in place and inspected.

02

Your menu in the CraveU app

Every student who opens the app sees your menu next to the other bays. Orders print at your station with the pickup time, so you cook in sequence, not in a rush.

03

A staffed pickup counter

Our team runs the counter, handles hand-offs and fixes any order issues on the spot. You never need a cashier.

04

Campus marketing

Student ambassadors, Instagram and TikTok, orientation and game-day promotions, and a featured spot in the app when you open.

05

Monthly sales & feedback

A dedicated point of contact, a monthly sales report by item and hour, and real student feedback so you can tune the menu.

06

Dining Dollars at checkout

We're working with UM to accept Dining Dollars and Cane Express, which puts your food in front of meal-plan budgets no off-campus shop can reach.

Pricing

Flat rent. No cut of your sales.

Most food halls take 15 to 30 percent of every order. We don't. Your bay rent covers the space and shared services, and diners pay CraveU a small $0.99 service fee per order. Every dollar on your menu is yours.

Who we're looking for

A deliberate mix, not a food court.

We fill bays so that campus gets real variety. A few national brands anchor steady demand. Most bays go to local Miami favorites that students would otherwise drive across town for.

  • Local restaurants with a proven menu and a loyal following
  • Fast-food or franchise operators looking for a campus unit at a fraction of a storefront's cost
  • Concepts that travel well in a bag: bowls, sandwiches, tacos, noodles, pizza, coffee
  • Operators who can run a station from 10:30 AM to 11 PM, or a breakfast or late-night shift
