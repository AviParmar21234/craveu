How pickup works

No dining room, no line, no table hunt. CraveU is built around the way a campus day actually runs: short gaps, long days and friends who never want the same thing.

Sign in with your CaneID

One tap with your UM login. That's how we know you're a student, faculty or staff, and how Dining Dollars and Cane Express show up as payment options.

Browse all 8 bays on one screen

Filter by craving, by "vegan," by "under $10" or by "ready in under 10 minutes." Every menu lives in one place, so you don't open five apps to decide on lunch.

Choose a pickup time

"ASAP" or a scheduled slot. Order at 1:10 PM during lecture and set pickup for 1:50 PM, right after class. We tell the bay when to start cooking so it's hot when you arrive.

Pay how you already pay

Dining Dollars, Cane Express, Apple Pay or card. A small CraveU service fee is shown on every order up front, with no surprises.

Get the "ready" text

Watch your order move from Received to Cooking to Ready. Your phone buzzes when it's at the counter.

Show your number, grab it, go

One pickup counter for all eight bays. Show the order code on your screen, take your bag, and you're back on the walk to class.

Honest pricing

Menu prices, plus one small service fee. That's it.

Restaurants set their own prices, the same as in their own shops. CraveU adds a flat $0.99 service fee per order to run the platform and the pickup counter. No delivery markups, no "small order" fees, no surge.

  • Same menu price you'd pay at the restaurant's own location
  • Fee shown before you pay, every time
  • Group orders: one fee per person's order, not per item

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to be a UM student?
No. Anyone can order. Students, faculty and staff sign in with their CaneID to unlock Dining Dollars and Cane Express. Visitors can check out as a guest with Apple Pay or a card.
Where is the pickup counter?
Inside the CraveU hall on Ponce de Leon Blvd, directly across from the Coral Gables campus. Walk in, look for the orange counter, show your order code.
Can I eat there?
CraveU is takeout-only. There are a few standing shelves for a quick bite, but it's designed for you to grab your food and take it to class, the library, the lake or your dorm.
What if my order is wrong?
Tap "Something wrong?" on the order screen and the counter team will fix it on the spot or refund the item to your original payment method. No forms, no email chains.
Do you deliver?
Not yet. We're focused on making pickup faster than anything else on campus. Dorm delivery is on the roadmap once bays are full.
