Mobile app prototype

A clickable walkthrough of the CraveU ordering app. Every screen maps to a block on our Business Model Canvas.

  1. Sign in with CaneID unlocks Dining Dollars & Cane Express UM partnership
  2. Browse all 8 bays on one screen with live wait times Value proposition
  3. Pick a pickup slot around your class schedule Takeout-only design
  4. Group order from different bays, everyone pays their own Group-order problem
  5. $0.99 service fee shown on every order Revenue stream #2
  6. Track & pick up at one counter, earn Crave Points Customer relationships

Tip: tap anything. Use the back arrow or the tab bar to move around. Reload to start over.
